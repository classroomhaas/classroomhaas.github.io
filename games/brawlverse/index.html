SETTINGS

ONLINE

Pick a fighter, then host a room or join with a code. Needs the game hosted on a normal website (e.g. GitHub Pages).

HOW TO PLAY

Move and jump (jump again in the air; tap down while falling to fast-fall). Attack + a direction gives tilts on the ground and aerials in the air; run + forward attack is a smash. Special + a direction gives four different specials; up special is your recovery, usable once per airtime. Hold shield to block (it wears down and breaks), shield + left/right dodges, shield + attack grabs and throws with the held direction. Damage raises your %, so hits launch you farther: get knocked past the blast zones and you lose a stock. Hits and damage fill the ultimate meter; at 100 press shield + special. Walk into item orbs: red bomb, yellow boots, blue prism shield, green spring.
